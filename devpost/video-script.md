## Tips
- Keep the total under 3 minutes with a few seconds to spare. Cut the evening-tablet part first if you run long.
- Record at 720p if your laptop is slow. Close other apps first.
- Upload to YouTube as Public (use Unlisted while checking it).
- Say "unproven on real glasses" out loud. It's the honest limit and the brief asks for it.
